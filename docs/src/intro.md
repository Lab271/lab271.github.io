[Lab271](https://schubergphilis.com/how-we-work/lab-271) is the innovation lab
of [Schuberg Philis](https://schubergphilis.com), a Netherlands-based
mission-critical IT company. The lab is named after its address, Boeing Avenue
271 in Schiphol-Rijk. 271 is also the first three digits of _e_, which is why
the growth curve keeps turning up in our branding.

This page indexes the public part of our work: the tooling, integrations and
experiments that come out of the lab. We write about them at
[stories.lab271.io](https://stories.lab271.io). Most Schuberg Philis open source
lives at [schubergphilis.github.io](https://schubergphilis.github.io/). The more
experimental tidbits, intern thesis work and lab plumbing live here.
